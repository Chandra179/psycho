Table values are rounded for display. Scores use a 0–1 scale internally and are shown out of 100. The expanded recorded details below retain full floating-point precision, formulas and operation order. Calculations clamp to 0–1, then round half away from zero to two decimals before and after calibration.

Each row shows a category's share of all words, multiplied by the model's weight to produce its contribution. The score also includes a baseline and, when configured, a calibration adjustment. Matched words are normalized examples, capped at 10 per category; the percentage uses all matches.

The rough score range is estimated from text length and dictionary coverage. It is not a percentile range or a statistically validated confidence interval.

Big Five scores use a correlation-weighted heuristic with assumed scaling. The other text measures and rough ranges are project-defined proxies. Research supports associations and constructs, not these particular prediction formulas. This is an experimental language-pattern estimate, not a validated individual personality measure. In a separate evaluation on 2,442 essays, trait-ranking AUC ranged from 0.524 to 0.554 across the five dimensions; 0.5 is chance.
